# City Infrastructure Designer

To design sewer lines, water supply, rainwater drains, roads, junctions or electricity cables
and have them checked, double-click **`start-app.bat`** (Windows) or **`start-app.sh`** (Mac and
Linux). Then follow the screen.

* How to use it: [docs/platform/USER_GUIDE.md](docs/platform/USER_GUIDE.md)
* Opening it in Visual Studio Code, and putting it on a website: [docs/platform/VSCODE_AND_WEBSITE.md](docs/platform/VSCODE_AND_WEBSITE.md)
* For developers: [backend/README.md](backend/README.md) and [docs/platform/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/platform/ARCHITECTURE.md)

The rest of this repository is the EPANET engine (see `README.md`), which the designer uses for water networks.
