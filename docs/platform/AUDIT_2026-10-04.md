# Independent audit – 4 October 2026

An independent reviewer (separate agent, not involved in writing the code) audited the whole
backend, the database schema and the help layer, re-computing results with its own scripts.
All 96 tests passed at the time; the reviewer still found the issues below. Every confirmed
finding has been fixed and is now covered by a regression test.

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | Electrical diversity compounded level by level; a transformer feeding one pillar could be reported below the load it carries (TX2 shown 91 % instead of 118 %) | Diversity now applied once to the sum of load maximum demands at each level, only where more than one load is served, and never below the largest outgoing feeder. Schedules show Σ load MD and the effective diversity. Tests `test_single_feeder_transformer_not_under_reported`, `test_single_load_not_diversified` |
| 2 | Major | GeoJSON without a CRS (or CRS84 / EPSG:4326) imported longitude/latitude as metres; `Project` accepted EPSG:4326 | Import refuses files with no CRS or a geographic CRS; `Project` refuses geographic CRSs (pyproj used when installed) |
| 3 | Major | An imported file could set objects to "approved" | Only `existing` / `proposed` can be imported; other statuses are reported and imported as `proposed` |
| 4 | Major | Sewer: no check that the diameter does not reduce downstream; no crown matching | `diameter_reduction` (FAIL) and `crown_matching` (WARNING) checks at every manhole |
| 5 | Major | Spiral–spiral curves got no minimum-radius check | Sharpest radius reached in a curve without an arc is checked against R_min |
| 6 | Major | Unsymmetrical vertical curves checked by total length; a one-sided curve passed | One-sided curves rejected; each leg checked through its equivalent symmetric length La(La+Lb)/Lb |
| 7 | Major | Water: storeys (2) and Hazen-Williams C (130) hard-coded | Storeys from the junction or the project rule `design_storeys`, otherwise NOT EVALUATED; roughness required |
| 8 | Major | Database: "frozen revision is immutable" not enforced; versions could overlap | Migration 0003: exclusion constraints on version ranges, triggers blocking edits/deletes/inserts in frozen revisions and un-freezing; verified in `verify_schema.sql` |
| 9 | Minor | Transition length omitted the empirical criterion Ls = 2.7·V²/R | Added as rule `transition_empirical_coeff` (by terrain, requires verification) |
| 10 | Minor | Junction: no major arm → angle check silently skipped; arm speed and design vehicle defaulted | NOT EVALUATED with a message; speed and design vehicle required |
| 11 | Minor | Report criteria tables omitted parameters actually used | Sewer, junction and electrical reports list them |
| 12 | Minor | Drainage travel time used a surcharged "velocity" Q/A_full; docs claimed an iteration | Travel time uses the full-section velocity at capacity, with a note; docs corrected |
| 13 | Minor | Silent defaults (drain lining, load category) and crashes on missing inputs | Inputs required and reported as errors |
| 14 | Minor | Unclosed polygon rings gave a wrong area | Rings closed automatically |
| 15 | Minor | Cross-sections and utility level checks ignored superelevation | Carriageway rotated to the design superelevation on curves, varying through transitions |
| 16 | Minor | A rule could be marked `verified` without a name | `verified_by` required |
| 17 | Minor | Wardrop capacity applied outside its range; weaving length measured on the outer circle | Validity ranges as a rule (`wardrop_validity`); outside them the capacity is NOT EVALUATED. Weaving length along the middle of the circulating carriageway |
| 18 | Note | LT current and drop used the 433 V no-load voltage | Uses project rule `lt_nominal_voltage_v` (415 V default, confirm with the licensee) |
| 19 | Note | Sag curves checked for headlight distance only | Comfort length 2√(A·v³/C) added (`sag_comfort_c_rate`) |
| 20 | Note | Two help texts could mislead (duration 0, roughness values) | Corrected |
| 21 | Note | Database: active-override uniqueness, current-row GiST index, check_result index | Added in migration 0003 |
| 22 | Note | Box-culvert capacity comment inaccurate | Corrected |

Test-quality points raised (tautological Kirpich and design-flow tests, untested paths) were
addressed: Kirpich is now checked against the original imperial form, the design flow against a
hand-worked number, and each finding above has its own test.

What the reviewer verified as correct is listed in its report: Manning partial-flow geometry,
Rational method, alternating-block hyetograph, EPANET and SWMM unit handling and input files,
clothoid and PI-curve geometry (against exact Fresnel integration), vertical curves, SSD,
R_min, transition criteria, junction kerb-return and roundabout geometry, electrical current and
voltage-drop formulae, and that reports carry the "not checked / not approved" banner.

## Re-verification

The same reviewer re-ran its scripts against the fixed code: findings 1–7, 9–14 and 16–22 FIXED;
8 and 15 partly fixed. It also found new issues introduced by the fixes, all now corrected:

| # | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Superelevation switched on in one step inside the transition (0.35 m jump at the edge) | Outer side now rotates −camber → +E and inner side −camber → −E linearly through the transition; normal camber kept where E ≤ camber. Test `test_superelevation_has_no_steps` walks the transition at 0.1 m intervals |
| N2 | Surcharged-drain travel time could understate downstream intensity | Travel time uses the faster of the capacity velocity and Q/A_full; the sheet shows "surcharged" instead of a velocity |
| N3 | Substation demand could exceed the sum of its outgoing cables | Node demand capped at the sum of outgoing demands |
| N4 | Rows could be inserted into, or updated into, a frozen revision; a frozen revision could be deleted | Triggers check the full revision range of new and updated rows; frozen revisions cannot be deleted or renumbered (tested locally; CI runs the same checks on PostGIS) |
| N5 | Road terrain defaulted to "plain" | Terrain required, with the allowed values listed in the error |
| N6 | Empirical transition length leaked into the reverse-curve tangent requirement | Reverse-curve tangent uses the superelevation run-off length only |
| N7 | Without pyproj some geographic codes passed; lon/lat values under a projected CRS were accepted | Larger list of geographic codes; imports whose coordinates all fall in longitude/latitude range are refused |
