Results are calculations to help an engineer. They are not an approved design.

What do you want to design?

Pick one. You can start from a ready-made example and change it, or draw your own on an empty sheet. Every button explains itself when you point at it.

How it works

  1. Draw – place points and join them, or draw the road, on the sheet.
  2. Fill in – a few simple details. Suggested values are filled in and marked so you can check them.
  3. Check – press Check my design. Green is fine, amber needs a look, red needs fixing, with advice on what to change.
  4. Share – download the report for the engineer.
